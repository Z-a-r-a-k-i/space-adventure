# Game audio

`sfx/*.ogg` and `cues.json` are generated by
[`tools/audio/build_game_audio.py`](../../tools/audio/build_game_audio.py). Every take is
processed (trimmed, layered, pitched, filtered, faded, peak-normalised) only from
the CC0 packs pinned by size and SHA-256 in
[`tools/audio/sources.json`](../../tools/audio/sources.json): Kenney Sci-Fi, Impact,
Interface and Digital Audio; rubberduck's *30 CC0 SFX loops* and *100 CC0 SFX*;
AntumDeluge's *Fire Crackling*. `cues.json` records each take's source layers.
Raw downloads stay in the ignored `artifacts/audio-sources/` cache.

`GameAudio` plays the manifest through `default_bus_layout.tres` (Master with a
limiter; Sfx, Ui, Ambience and a reserved Music bus). The station uses the
`station.*` cues positionally; the ship battle pans player-side cues left and
enemy-side cues right. Take choice and pitch variation are presentation-only.
There is no music yet. Owner listening acceptance remains open; the builder also
writes an ignored audition montage under `artifacts/audio-review/`.
