## Purpose

This pack explores two deliberately different large-warship directions within
the existing SpaceAdventure visual language:

- a rugged frontier command carrier that reads as a mobile fortress, carrier,
  refugee ark, and repairable inhabited machine; and
- an ominous siege dreadnought with a directional spear silhouette, recessed
  spinal accelerator, blockade massing, and restrained hostile lighting.

Both extend the escape cutter's chunky dark-shell construction, broad bevels,
purposeful cyan or red-orange lighting, matte industrial materials, and
post-collapse repair language to ships several kilometres long. They combine
retro-futurist survival engineering, restrained cyberpunk technology, and
space-adventure scale without copying an existing franchise.

These images do not expand the bounded Phase 7 ship-combat proof of concept.
They do not imply fleet combat, capital-ship command, playable capital ships,
factions, weapon rules, production asset IDs, or an implementation phase.

## Review boundary

Review:

- whether both ships look several kilometres long rather than like enlarged
  cutters;
- whether they feel related to the existing escape cutter without repeating
  its silhouette;
- whether the command carrier reads as inhabited, maintained, modular, and
  formidable rather than luxurious;
- whether the siege dreadnought reads as hostile, directional, and functional
  without relying on spikes, skulls, Gothic ornament, or a copied triangular
  silhouette;
- whether tiny escorts, hangars, windows, orbital docks, and the planetary
  backgrounds communicate scale without hiding either capital ship; and
- whether restrained cyan/amber and red-orange treatments preserve the
  project's player/neutral/hostile color logic.

Approval of an image would authorize only a visual direction. It would not
define factions, ownership, interiors, crew count, weapon behavior, fleet
rules, travel, encounters, or production scope. Any model-ready capital ship
would require a later roster decision, approved reference, accepted
production-ready brief, assigned ownership, and normal Blender and Godot
review.
