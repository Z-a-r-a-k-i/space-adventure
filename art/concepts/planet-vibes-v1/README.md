## Purpose

This pack explores three possible later SpaceAdventure locations. Each image
combines one environment, one friendly NPC direction, and two readable enemy
archetypes in a single landscape decision draft:

- a humid alien jungle wrapped around an abandoned research outpost;
- a windswept desert surrounding a buried fuel stop and relay; and
- a rain-darkened lower district on a planet-spanning city.

The requested Fallout, cyberpunk, and space-adventure mixture is interpreted
as chunky post-collapse retro-futurism, restrained practical neon, lived-in
survival gear, adventurous off-world scale, and original silhouettes. The
images intentionally avoid copied characters, logos, power armor, vault
clothing, mascots, weapons, environments, and interface elements.

The pack preserves the frontier-station baseline's elevated tactical view,
broad low-poly forms, dark industrial construction, readable silhouettes, and
purposeful color semantics. Amber identifies the featured NPC direction,
red-orange identifies threats, and cyan remains a restrained technology color.

## Review boundary

Review:

- whether the three locations feel related to the existing frontier station
  while having distinct climate, architecture, and atmosphere;
- whether the friendly NPC reads clearly without looking like a fourth party
  kit;
- whether both enemy archetypes remain distinct from the NPC and from one
  another at an elevated tactical view;
- whether the jungle balances alien nature with decayed machinery;
- whether the desert balances survivalist retro-futurism with off-world scale;
  and
- whether the city uses cyberpunk density without losing the playable route
  or becoming neon noise.

Approval of an image would authorize only a visual direction. It would not
define a planet's lore, faction, dialogue eligibility, quests, traversal,
combat behavior, attacks, abilities, encounter count, production asset IDs, or
implementation order. Offline 3D production requires a later roster decision,
approved reference, accepted production-ready brief, assigned ownership, and
the normal licensing, privacy, Blender, and Godot gates.
