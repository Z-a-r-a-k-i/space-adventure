# Ship hazard markers

Original project-owned dimensional Blender geometry; no provider assets or external textures.
See [the ship brief](../../briefs/ship-battle-v1.md) for scope and `manifest.json`
for dimensions, fresh-import checks and reproduction. The published GLB has
floor origin, Godot +Y up, unit scale, and no gameplay or autonomous clock.

Fresh exports were inspected in Blender and in an overhead Godot capture with
the cutter and full-scale crew. Fire cores remain visible through translucent
outer tongues. The breach aperture uses an unlit dark material so it remains
readable under the ship's overhead lighting. Runtime activation and work motion
are adapter responsibilities. Owner visual acceptance remains pending.
